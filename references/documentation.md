# Proportional documentation

## Light
Individual projects, experiments and small apps. Keep README, PROJECT-STATE.json and HANDOFF.md. Create ADRs for consequential decisions only.

## Professional
Maintained products and team work: add requirements, architecture, decision log, test evidence, changelog and release record where applicable.

## Critical
Higher-risk or regulated projects: add risk register, requirements traceability, security assessment, backup/restore evidence and independent reviews as appropriate. This does not constitute a certification.

## Provenance
Claim status: verified (primary evidence checked); reported (stakeholder statement); inferred (analysis); unknown. Include source locator and time when relevant.

## Lifecycle
Draft → In review → Approved → Superseded/Archived. Preserve history; do not rewrite earlier decisions. Prefer concise, linked documentation.
